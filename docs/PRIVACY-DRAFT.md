**Last updated: [date]**

Groupcade ("we") makes party games you play on phones and a TV at groupcade.com and in the
Groupcade app. This policy explains what we collect and what you can do about it.

## Players

You can play without an account. When you join a game you type a name. We keep that name, your
answers and your score only while the room is open, and delete them 2 hours after the room's last
activity. We keep anonymous game statistics (for example, how many people played and how many
rounds), which may include the winner's name.

## Hosts with an account

Accounts are optional and only for hosts. If you sign in with Apple, Google or an emailed code, we
keep:

- your name and email address (with Apple, this can be a private relay address),
- the ID Apple or Google gives us for your account,
- your saved game settings, your question packs, and a history of the games you hosted (who
  played, final places and the winner),
- whether you asked to hear about new games, and
- the devices you're signed in on (so you can sign out of them).

We use this only to run your account and show it back to you. We don't sell it, share it with
advertisers or track you across other apps and sites. If you ask to hear about new games, we email
you when one comes out; every email has an unsubscribe link.

## Deleting your account

You can delete your account at any time in the app (Your account > Delete account) or at
groupcade.com/account/delete/. We delete your account, settings, question packs and game history
right away. Anonymous game statistics stay, without your name or anyone's scores. If you signed
in with Apple, we also tell Apple to end Groupcade's access to your Apple ID.

## Other things

- We use Apple and Google only to confirm who you are when you sign in.
- We use an email service to send sign-in codes.
- The site is hosted by Pressable in the United States.
- Hosts must be 13 or older. Players don't need an account, and we don't knowingly collect personal
  information from children.
